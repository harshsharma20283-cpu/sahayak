# Business model

## Core rule

**The citizen never pays.** Sahayak is free for the person seeking help, and every scheme application is free.

## Who pays

| Payer | Why they pay |
|---|---|
| CSR funds | District impact reports for their programmes |
| NGOs | Screening dashboard for their field workers |
| e-Mitra / CSC operators | Faster and more accurate screening at their centre (sponsor-paid) |
| State government and banks | Higher uptake of schemes, outreach insight, fewer middleman complaints |

## Pilot

- 2 NGOs
- 10 e-Mitra centres
- 1 district

## Scaling

- Add a new state's scheme data.
- Use the same rule engine.

Note: all impact numbers in the current build are SAMPLE. No pilot results exist yet.
