# Pitch

## 3-minute script

**0:00 - Kamla's story**
Kamla Devi is 62, a widow, in a village near Jaipur. A middleman comes and asks for Rs 500 to "get her pension". The scheme application is free. She does not know that.

**0:30 - Speak in Hindi**
Kamla speaks in Hindi. Her profile appears on screen. The app asks one follow-up question. Then it shows the results: which schemes she is likely eligible for, and why.

**1:15 - Open a scheme**
Open the widow pension. Show the rules, the checklist, the apply steps, and the official link. Show the verification status (needs_verification). Show the FREE alert: "do not pay anyone".

**1:50 - Admin changes a rule**
Log in to /admin. Change an income limit. Go back to the citizen view. The result changes. No redeploy.

**2:20 - Tracker and impact**
Show the application tracker and a reminder card. Show the impact dashboard, clearly labelled SAMPLE.

**2:45 - Business model and ask**
The citizen never pays. CSR, NGOs, e-Mitra centres, and government and banks pay for reports, dashboards and outreach. Ask: a pilot in one district with 2 NGOs and 10 e-Mitra centres.

## 8-slide outline

1. Title: Sahayak - सहायक (Claim What's Yours). One-line pitch.
2. Problem: schemes are unclear, middlemen charge money for free applications.
3. Kamla Devi: target user and her day.
4. How it works: speak, confirm, follow-up, results, track.
5. Why it is explainable: rule engine with met / not_met / unknown, and the reason for each result.
6. Guardrails: likely eligible wording, no ID fields, official sources, free alert.
7. Demo status: what is built, what is not done (no live LLM check, no real SMS, phone voice not tested).
8. Business model and district pilot ask.
