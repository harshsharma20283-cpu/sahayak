# Pitch

Sahayak: Apna haq, apni bhasha mein. A plain-language scheme finder for Rajasthan's widows and senior citizens.

## 3-minute demo script

| Time | Scene |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Kamla's story. A middleman asks her for Rs 500 to file her pension form. |
| 0:30 | Kamla speaks in Hindi. Her profile appears. One follow-up question. Results are shown. |
| 1:15 | Open a scheme. Show rules, document checklist, application steps, official link, verified date and the FREE alert. |
| 1:50 | Admin changes an income limit. Kamla's result changes on the next check. |
| 2:20 | Tracker, reminder card, and impact dashboard, with its assumptions shown. |
| 2:45 | Business model and the ask: a district pilot. |

## 8-slide outline

1. Title and promise
2. Problem: conflicting sources on scheme rules
3. Who we serve: widows and senior citizens in Rajasthan
4. Solution flow, with 3 screenshots
5. Rules as data, LLM for language, visible reasoning
6. Trust and guardrails
7. Business model and impact
8. Roadmap and team
