# Business Model

The citizen never pays. Sahayak is free to use, and no agent or centre may charge citizens for it.

## Payers

| Payer | What they pay for |
|---|---|
| CSR funds | District impact reports; annual sponsorship |
| NGOs | Screening dashboard, as a subscription or grant-funded licence |
| e-Mitra and CSC operators | Faster, more accurate screening. Sponsor-paid licence, never citizen-paid. |
| State government and banks | Higher uptake, drop-off insight, fewer middleman complaints. Pilot contract. |

## Go-to-market

Pilot with 2 NGOs and 10 e-Mitra centres in one district. Measure applications started and applications approved.

## Scaling

Add another state's scheme data file. The engine stays unchanged.
